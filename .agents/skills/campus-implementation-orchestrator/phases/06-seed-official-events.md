# Phase 6 — Seed Official Events

## Objective
Provide 4–5 realistic official campus events so the product is demonstrable immediately after setup.

Include examples such as:
- Full Stack Web Development Workshop
- AI/ML Career Conference
- 24-Hour Hackathon
- Photography Walk
- Startup & Entrepreneurship Meetup

Use realistic dates, times, locations, descriptions, categories, and verified organizations where appropriate.

## Acceptance criteria
- Seed data is reproducible.
- Seeded records are visible through the existing Luma UI.
- Seeds do not bypass production authorization.
